# Note jar: praise, encouragement and a little sass

Implemented September 16, 2026. This replaces the original jar writing. The library contains **340 original notes**, including **15 for each of the eight feelings**, **20 affectionate tough-love notes**, and **20 clearly labelled Maomao-inspired notes**.

## What changed

The earlier writing leaned toward permission and gentle advice. Josh asked for direct praise, encouragement, teasing, sarcasm and occasional tough love in normal, casual language. The new centre of gravity is **liking Kiriya as a person**: her smile, humour, kindness, creative skills and particular interests. Reminders support that affection instead of turning every pull into another task.

Examples from the actual library:

- “ur art is cool. yes, even the piece ur about to explain the flaws in. let me like it for a second.”
- “u have a kind heart and a very active commentary track. i like both. the combination is excellent.”
- “maomao has herbs. miku has songs. u have an ever-expanding list of things to get very normal about. obviously.”
- “being nervous doesn't cancel being capable. the artist, dancer and cosplayer in u are still there.”
- “save the file before 'one more tiny change'. i believe in ur art. i do not believe in trusting a battery percentage.”

These are original site notes, signed **✦ kiriya.love**. They are not represented as messages written by Josh, quotations from a character, or live observations of her behaviour.

## Research and access limits

The requested Deep Research skill was searched for in installed skills, plugin caches and the workspace; its earlier package is no longer present and no callable research-specific tool was available. Research used the web tool directly. No plugin was installed.

Direct TikTok access was blocked by robots rules. Instagram searches did not expose usable live comment threads, and an artist's linked Instagram post could not be opened. Accordingly this is **a source-informed editorial study, not a scrape of current teen conversations or a claim to have watched inaccessible Reels**. Evidence includes a preserved creator caption, an Instagram artist's own commentary guide, platform reports and linguistic research. Generic SEO lists of thousands of compliments and shop search pages were excluded.

## Mood and energy

| Saved feeling | What the note should do |
| --- | --- |
| Sad | Offer affection and direct reassurance; no demand to smile, improve or become productive. |
| Anxious | Remind her of her capability and give room for uncertainty; no teasing about symptoms. |
| Overwhelmed | Reduce demands, keep praise simple, remind her she matters beyond output. |
| Angry | Respect space and boundaries; dry Maomao side-eye humour is okay. Encourage a pause before replying without assuming she is right about an unknown dispute. |
| Happy | Share her enjoyment and praise skills, personality and small wins. |
| Excited | Invite enthusiasm about a character, song, cosplay idea or drawing; don't immediately turn the idea into a deadline. |
| Content | Quiet appreciation and ordinary pleasures; no pressure to turn peaceful into productive. |
| Playful | More affectionate exaggeration, fandom teasing, sass and short punchlines. |

The separate **tough** family gives specific, manageable nudges: save the file, be fair to a rough draft, let a compliment land. It only participates for happy/excited/content/playful moods with battery **2–4**. The **humour** family excludes sad/anxious/overwhelmed/angry and requires battery **2–4**. Playful-specific cards also require battery **2–4**. Low battery favours brief care and rest while keeping genuine praise available.

A saved mood is an **eligibility rule** for mood-tagged notes, not merely a ranking hint. Recent topics are varied within that suitable pool. Without a check-in the full range is available at ordinary battery, with general notes preferred; the picker remains optional. The app does not infer emotions from presentation, private messages, a photo or listening habits.

## Variety, history and verification

- 340 independently written notes, 19 themes; all text and content-derived IDs are distinct. The current texts are 72–141 characters, comfortable for a phone-sized note.
- No repeat while unread notes compatible with the current mood/battery remain. When that pool is exhausted, it cycles with the existing **revisited** label. Immediate repeats are excluded. This avoids forcing cheerful or tough-love text into a sad moment just to exhaust the entire library.
- A pool reset clears only its eligible IDs from the current cycle, retaining seen IDs for other moods. Permanent seen IDs and delivered snapshots remain intact.
- Existing saved/delivered notes keep their original wording. Rewritten notes receive new content-derived IDs and become eligible as new writing. No database migration or destructive history rewrite is involved.
- No live AI calls or external transmission of mood data; research informs a reviewed server-side library.

Validation: note-jar regression tests cover uniqueness, all eight moods, low/normal/high battery, exhaustion, mood switching, unlimited pulls, concurrency/idempotency, favourites, dated snapshots and birthday return. Browser checks and the production build are recorded in the implementation handoff.
