# Official Miku and Maomao wallpapers for the private chat background

> **2026-11-27, round 4:** Josh replaced the Halloween 2025 Maomao (he read her tool as a scythe)
> with the season 1 episode 6 wallpaper gift, Maomao made up for the garden party
> (`season1/special/img/wall/06/03.jpg`), cropped to its three panels: the dark band at the top and
> the logo/© band at the bottom removed, so the © line now shows in the chat and Art & credits.

Researched 2026-11-24. Research only: nothing in the repo was changed. For inspection, images were downloaded to the session scratchpad (`dl-sonicwire/`, `dl-piapro/`, `dl-kusuriya/`, `view/`) and not into the repo. Dimensions were read from the real files or from live byte-range requests. This is not legal advice.

**Use case.** A non-commercial background in a private web app and iPhone app built by one person for his partner. Two people use it, and the images are not redistributed.

| Maomao | The official anime site (Toho / 製作委員会) offers "スマホ用待ち受け" (smartphone wallpaper) downloads, 2048×4432 or 1125×2434 | **No written terms.** The downloads are only labelled as smartphone wallpapers, and the umbrella Toho terms prohibit copying or public transmission without permission. | **Probably**, only if served privately behind auth. **No** if publicly reachable or bundled in a public App Store build. |

**Plainly: no official Maomao source with written, permissive personal-use terms exists.** The official site gives away genuine phone-shaped wallpapers, but it states no terms at all. Of the official channels checked, it is the only active free source.

Translation of the key sentence: outside of video use and hand-copying, for non-commercial, free use you may use the official images as-is or modified, provided you always display the credit below. Distribution aimed at an unspecified large audience is not allowed.

**How this applies here.** The app is non-commercial (非営利), free (無償) and used by two people, so it is not 不特定多数. Resizing, cropping and putting the art on a backdrop count as "改変", which is allowed. **Verdict: yes.** The credit line is mandatory. Show it next to the background picker or on a credits screen:

> Q11 … please do not distort, mutilate, modify or take other derogatory action in relation to the Characters that would be prejudicial to Crypton's honor or reputation … examples of prohibited uses include use in an overly violent context or in a sexual context.

**Verdict: yes.** Recommended credit for a cropped or adapted use: "features an adaptation of Hatsune Miku, © Crypton Future Media, Inc. 2007, licensed under a CC BY-NC: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/".

The CC grant clearly covers only the "original illustrations" shown on that page. The direct image is `https://piapro.net/intl/images/popup_miku.jpg`, 600×600. For the V3, V4X, NT and V6 product art, rely on §E(3) above, not on CC.

**C. Project SEKAI (SEGA / Colorful Palette)** — https://pjsekai.sega.jp/guideline/index.html

## 2. Maomao (薬屋のひとりごと)

### 2a. What the official site offers

`kusuriyanohitorigoto.jp` sits behind a Cloudflare challenge for HTML pages, so the pages were read from Wayback Machine captures. The **image files themselves are publicly served** (live HTTP 200/206 on 2026-11-24). Three official giveaways exist:

1. **"猫猫、壬氏と巡る四季" monthly visual series**, released every 22nd (猫猫の日), July 2024 to June 2026. It has now ended; Dengeki Online (2026-06-22) says "2年間お届けしたこのシリーズも、今月でついに最後の一枚となります。" Each gallery page has two links: "**スマホ用待ち受け**" (smartphone wallpaper) and "**PC用待ち受け**" (PC wallpaper). Press coverage: "公式サイトでは、今回のビジュアルを使ったPC、スマホの壁紙を配布しています。" (Dengeki Online, 2026-03-22). The phone files are designed for a phone. The illustration sits in the middle third, with a patterned band above and the Kusuriya wordmark and © line below.
   - Page pattern: `https://kusuriyanohitorigoto.jp/season2/gallery/seasonvisual_<id>.php`
   - Phone file pattern: `https://kusuriyanohitorigoto.jp/season2/assets/img/gallery/wall/seasonvisual_<id>_sp.jpg` (`_pc.jpg` for PC)
2. **Event visuals**: Tanabata, Halloween 2024 and 2025, New Year, Valentine's and White Day. Pages are `…/season2/gallery/eventvisual_<id>.php`, with phone files in the same `/wall/` folder.
3. **Per-episode "待ち受けプレゼント" (wallpaper gift)**:
   - Season 1, episodes 1–24: `https://kusuriyanohitorigoto.jp/season1/special/img/wall/<ep>/<n>.jpg`, 1125×2434. These were originally at `/special/img/wall/…`, which is now a 404.
   - Season 2, episodes 25–48: `https://kusuriyanohitorigoto.jp/season2/special/wallpaper/<ep>/<n>.jpg`, about 2048×4431. The index is `…/season2/special/wallpaper.php`.
   - These are scene stills, so later episodes carry story spoilers.

Season 3 (episodes 49–51 so far) and the film site have no wallpaper files at the season-2 path pattern (404). They may appear later.

### 2b. Terms (exact wording found)

- **On the wallpaper pages themselves**, read via Wayback captures of `special/wallpaper.html`, `season2/special/wallpaper.php` and the `seasonvisual_*.php` / `eventvisual_*.php` pages: **no usage terms at all.** They show only the link labels "スマホ用待ち受け" / "PC用待ち受け", the special-page tile "待ち受けプレゼント", and the footer "©日向夏・イマジカインフォス／「薬屋のひとりごと」製作委員会". The footer links only to Toho's privacy and cookie policies.
- **Toho's general terms (ご利用条件)**, https://www.toho.co.jp/term_of_use/ (updated 2026-09-01), formally cover toho.co.jp. They show the publisher's stance:
  > 本サイトに含まれるすべての…イラスト、映像、写真…に係る著作権は、東宝またはそれぞれの著作権者に帰属しています。これらの著作物の全部もしくは一部を、東宝もしくは著作権者の許諾を得ずに複製し、または公衆送信（送信可能化を含みます。）するなどの行為により利用することは、**あらかじめこれを許諾する旨の表示がある場合および著作権法により特に認められる場合を除き**、東宝または著作権者の著作権を侵害する行為に当たります。

**Interpretation:**
- The "スマホ用待ち受け" label is the only "許諾する旨の表示" (indication of permission). It plausibly grants personal use as a phone wallpaper.
- Showing the image as a chat background in a private app used only by the two of you is functionally the same kind of private display. Copying within a household or similarly limited circle is also generally private use under Japanese copyright law (Art. 30).
- The risk is **公衆送信 / 送信可能化** (making the file available to the public). That happens if the file is served from a public URL, such as the repo's `public/` folder, or shipped in a public App Store build.

**Verdict: probably**, provided all of the following hold:
- the image is served only behind the app's authentication (not from `public/`) and the iOS app is not publicly distributed;
- the art is kept intact apart from crop and resize, and the © line or an equivalent credit stays visible;
- it is not redistributed.

The cleanest variant: the app offers a "use your own background" upload, and she downloads the official wallpaper from the official site herself.

## 3. Implementation caveats (apply to whichever is picked)

1. **Keep character art out of `public/`.** Serve it through the authenticated media route the site already uses for private media. For Maomao this is what separates "probably fine" from "public transmission". For Miku it keeps the image clearly within "not distributed to the general public".
2. **The iOS app:** bundling the images is fine for an ad-hoc, TestFlight or personal-team install on two phones. It is **not** fine for a public App Store listing, which would distribute the art to the public. Fetching from the authenticated backend avoids the question.
3. **Credits:**
   - Miku (§E): `「初音ミク」はクリプトン・フューチャー・メディア株式会社の著作物です。 © Crypton Future Media, INC. www.piapro.net`
   - Miku (CC route, `miku.webp`): the existing CC BY-NC line.
   - Maomao: keep the baked-in "©日向夏・イマジカインフォス／「薬屋のひとりごと」製作委員会" visible, or repeat it in a credits note, and link the official gallery page.
4. **Edits:** cropping, resizing, blur or tint overlays for readability count as "改変" under Crypton §E (allowed, but any rights in the modified image stay with Crypton) and must not be derogatory (CC Q11). For Maomao no edit permission is stated, so prefer minimal edits: crop/scale and a translucent scrim under bubbles, with the art itself unaltered.
5. **Hotlinking:** the official image URLs work today but can change; Season 1 files already moved once. If hotlinking, expect breakage. If copying to the private store, record the source page URL and the retrieval date, as ASSETS.md already does.
6. **Spoilers:** the per-episode wallpapers from later Season 2 episodes show story scenes; the seasonal and event visuals are spoiler-free.
