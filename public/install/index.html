your little world, as a real app on your phone ♡

Get TestFlight first
  1. Install TestFlight, Apple’s own app for private apps (the button above).
  2. Open the email from Apple inviting you to kiriya.love on your iPhone, and tap View in TestFlight → Accept → Install.
  3. In TestFlight, keep Automatic Updates on, so new versions arrive by themselves.
  4. Open kiriya.love and type your passphrase once. After that, Face ID opens it.
  5. Say Allow to notifications, so little notes reach you.
The little orange dot next to its name just means it came from TestFlight.
