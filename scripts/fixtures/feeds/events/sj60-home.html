Celebrating 60 Years of
Japan-Singapore

An Interactive Festival of Culture and Technology

Experience through music, performances, anime, games, tourism, food culture, and cutting-edge technology. Visitors can explore unique culture, lifestyle, and innovations, fostering mutual understanding and celebrating the partnership between Japan and Singapore.
Corporate and government booths offer opportunities to experience the latest products and services firsthand. The festival is designed for all ages, making it enjoyable for families, friends, and communities alike.
The event features four zones (Performance, Culture, Tourism & Food, and Technology) as well as an F&B area with eat-in space.
