Booth Placement Policies

Guaranteed corner placement and placement further from the stage, nearer to the entrance are purchaseable options, which can be added anytime before we begin to finalise the event layout, which is usually approximately 1 month before the event day. 

By default, booth placements are determined by the order of payment received within each designated booth type (i.e. wall/standard/cosplay/corner), with early payments securing spots further from the stage. The assignment of corner booths is random and determined by sequence of payments.
You may request adjacent placement to other successfully registered booths. A separate email will be sent about this nearer to the event.
Reminder: This is a music festival with sound levels that go up to 90dB. Regardless of your booth location, it is impossible to guarantee a quiet environment. Consider bringing ear plugs and writing materials for comfort and communication.
Content Policies
Prohibited Items & Activities:
– Items prohibited under Singaporean law (e.g., weapons, chemicals, seditious materials, stolen goods).
– Adult (18+) materials.
– Unauthorized Goods: You are solely responsible for obtaining all necessary licenses and rights for the items you sell or display. Plagiarized content, counterfeit goods, and unlicensed merchandise are strictly prohibited.

– Deceptive practices (e.g., misrepresenting AI-generated or traced art as original).
– Games with gambling elements (e.g., gacha, mystery boxes) must comply with the Gambling Control Act.
– Risqué or overly revealing displays. Please be mindful that children will be attending the event.

We reserve the right to request removal of inappropriate items. Violation of these guidelines may result in expulsion from the event without refund, blacklisting from future events, and/or a police report being filed.

Policy on AI-Generated Works

While we understand that AI can be a valuable assistive tool for artists and businesses, the sale of products containing visuals that are generated by AI with little or no human intervention is currently not widely accepted by the creative community. Thus, we are unable to permit the sale of such products.

Enforcement Against Sale of Prohibited Content

While we cannot preemptively screen all content, we take reports of prohibited content seriously and investigate them based on provided evidence. This includes products sold at the event and graphics used in booth promotional materials. Reports should include specific details and supporting evidence (e.g., links to original work, image overlays). Those accused of violations may be asked to provide additional information (e.g., work-in-progress files). If investigations cannot be conducted or concluded before the event, they continue after the event, and confirmed violations may result in blacklisting from future events.

Post-Payment Terms and Conditions

Contract and Receipt Issuance

  • The payment acknowledgement email will be sent and it confirms the contract between the event and the applicants based on the terms and conditions listed here.
  • If needed for accounting purposes, a PDF receipt is available upon request after the completion of payment.

Changing booth categories or adding additional tables and other options after payment confirmation

  • Upgrading to a higher-value booth is possible, subject to availability. Please contact us for assistance.
  • Applicants cannot downgrade from a booth of a higher value to a booth of a lower value. However, it is possible to transfer your booth to another creator. Please contact us for assistance.

Cancellation, Transfer and Refund Policy

  • Once your registration is confirmed, cancellations or refunds are generally not permitted.

  • Cancellations and Refunds for add-ons are not possible. 

  • In exceptional cases, we may make partial refunds for booths if the exhibitor is unable to be present, depending on the amount of lead time running up to the event. 

  • To prevent any losses, you are encouraged to transfer your booth to another eligible individual. Please provide us with the new exhibitor’s information, and we will send you the booth transfer form once the transfer is approved.

  • In the event of unforeseen circumstances necessitating the postponement of the festival, such as national directives, your booth registration will be automatically transferred to the rescheduled date. This aligns with the protocol established by our event venue concerning rentals, and we appreciate your understanding in this matter.

Transferring or Loaning Exhibitor Passes

  • Transferring your exhibitor pass to another individual or creator without prior written permission is strictly prohibited. 

  • Loaning your exhibitor pass to anyone for the purpose of granting them entry to the event is strictly prohibited.

  • Any unauthorised transfer or loan of an exhibitor pass will result in the immediate revocation of access rights for both the pass holder and the recipient. No refunds will be issued in such cases.

  • Involvement in unauthorised transfer or loaning will lead to blacklisting from future EOY events.

Renting or subletting of booth

You may not rent out or sublet your booth for profit. Any violation of this policy will result in the withdrawal of exhibitor rights without refund, and may lead to being banned from future events.

Booth Space Usage Regulations

Access for Booth Set-Up

Early Access for booth set-up: To be announced. It will be on both mornings of the event days. Set-up before the event day is not permitted.

Booth Set-Up and Safety

  • Front Setup & Height Limits: The official height limit for your front table setup is 140cm (measured from the ground). You may build higher than this limit, provided your setup is secure and stable. All displays and products must be firmly attached to the table. However, if your setup appears unstable or in danger of toppling, we reserve the right to enforce the strict 140cm limit for safety.

  • Wall Height Limits: Items placed against the venue walls must not exceed 200cm in height from the ground.

  • Backings & Frames: Standard booths may not have a display frame or backing behind them. These are only permitted for booths with walls.

  • Furniture & Space: Please use the provided tables and chairs. You may bring your own additional furniture, but it must remain entirely within your designated booth space.

  • Tools & Equipment: The use of power tools and ladders is restricted to authorized professionals. Anyone found using this equipment during setup will be instructed to stop immediately.

  • Damages: Exhibitors are responsible for any charges related to venue damage or adhesive residue removal. Tip: If there is existing damage at your booth space, please inform the event staff before you start setting up to prevent any misunderstandings later.

  • Safety & Liability: All items must be secured. Any collapse of products or setups resulting in injury to event-goers is the sole liability of the exhibitor. We reserve the right to request the removal of any items that do not adhere to these safety regulations.

Booth Activities

  • With the exception of Cosplay and Meet & Greet booths that come with queue space, you must ensure that the walkway in front of your booth is kept clear at all times. You may not have sustained queues stretching beyond your booth lasting more than 5 minutes. Exceeding this time limit will result in additional floor space charges.
  • Be considerate of neighbouring booths. You will be asked to stop your activities if they become disruptive. 

Maintenance, cleanliness and food

  • Do not bring strongly scented food into the event.
  • You are responsible for maintaining the cleanliness of your booth area. A cleaning fee of $50 will be charged in the event of any spills. 

Booth tear down

You don’t need to pack up your booth at the end of the first day, but you must start packing up your booth by the tear-down time (TBA) on the second day.

Overnight storage of property, loss and damage policy

  • Due to the large crowds anticipated throughout the event, take extra precautions for your merchandise and belongings. While our event staff strives to ensure a safe overall environment, they cannot monitor individual booths. We highly recommend not leaving your booth unattended during the event.
  • You may store your merchandise overnight at your booth. However, please take all valuables with you, and conceal your merchandise with a tablecloth and/or keep it under the table skirting.
  • We are not liable and unable to compensate for any missing, misplaced or damaged items. If you experience any loss, damage, or suspicious activity, we can assist you in contacting venue security to file a report. However, you would need to file a police report yourself.
