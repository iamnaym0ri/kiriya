About Anime Festival Asia

Anime Festival Asia (AFA) is a convention that unites Japanese pop culture fans across South East Asia by showcasing the latest and most current trends direct from Japan.

Since its inception in 2008, AFA has delivered dozens of stellar festival experiences in 18 years, making it the premier Japan Pop Culture convention in the region.

AFA Singapore 2023 received the Outstanding Leisure Event Award at the Singapore Tourism Awards 2024.

AFA Singapore also won this award in 2019 and 2023, having been a finalist in the category from 2017 to 2020* and 2023 to 2024.


*AFA was not held in 2020 to 2022 due to COVID-19, and was not nominated for the Best Leisure Event category

AFA's Official Mascots

Introducing the faces of AFA, Seika and AFAkun are AFA’s beloved mascots who appear in our events’ key visuals.
Learn more about them below!

Seika

Introduced in 2014, Seika is Anime Festival Asia’s official mascot. She is seen in the key visual of every AFA as well as other regional events such as AFA Creators Super Fest and AFA Station. Seika’s trademark is her bright pink twintail hairstyle. She is illustrated by many popular artists around the world for AFA’s different events!

AFAkun

AFAkun is Anime Festival Asia’s official character mascot and is often seen in key visuals and merchandise collaterals. AFAkun’s trademarks would be his striking red colour suit, the “A” on his chest and his headphones.

Exhibition Zones

An all-in-one showcase of Japanese Pop Culture!
Check out what’s in store in AFA’s Exhibition Hall.

Our massive exhibition hall is home to everything Japan Pop Culture!

From showcases of the latest anime titles, to exhibitions of the biggest names, AKIBA TOWN offers merchandise, services, immersive interactions for fans to support their favourites, learn about new trends, upcoming releases and have fun doing so!

Where the future takes center stage!

The evolution of Akiba Stage into AFA NEXT STAGE reflects our commitment to spotlighting what’s new, upcoming, and trending in anime, cosplay, gaming, and pop culture.

It’s the launchpad for fresh creators, innovative content, and the next big things fans will be talking about.

CREATORS HUB is a dedicated by fans, for fans community area within AFA’s exhibition hall.

Renowned creators that include illustrators, crafters and more, will exhibit their self-published products of fan merchandise for fellow fans.

COSPLAY HUB is a dedicated area for regional cosplayers to exhibit their self-produced cosplayer merchandise, as well as organise their own meet and greet sessions with fans.

What is culture without the flavours and textures related to it?

At FOOD STREET, the booths lined up offer a wide range of hearty snacks or light bites for those who want a taste of Japan. Traditional, fusion or even novelty trends, all is welcome here.

With the rise of gaming as an interest and bonding activity, AFA has created a new zone dedicated to all things related to games.

Here you will find a stage for showcase and friendly play, as well as the latest editions of beloved titles and indie games. Join us at GameOn!

Day Stage

Guest talk shows, premiere screenings, and exclusive contents!

DAY STAGE is set in a hall of its own, with seats for an exclusive and comfortable experience.

This stage is where the biggest names in the industry feature the work that goes into their latest titles, hold premiere screenings, special showcases and dialogues to engage with fans.

I Love Anisong Concert

A celebration of Japanese Pop music and artistes!

Created in 2009, this brand is unique to SOZO. “Anisong” is a coined word popularised by AFA to celebrate “anime songs” within the region and was created to establish AFA’s concert component.

Since then, it has brought in the largest amount of Japanese music artistes in Asia for nights of rousing performances.

Besides “I Love Anisong”, AFA’s concert component has also hosted music labels and exclusive themed concerts where the line-up and song list is curated for a most exhilarating experience.
