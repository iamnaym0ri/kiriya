✨ JAPAN EXPO THAILAND 2027 — 12th Edition ✨

Be part of one of Thailand’s biggest Japan-related events of the year, celebrating the 140th Anniversary of Thailand–Japan Diplomatic Relations.

We welcome brands, companies, organizations, businesses, and creators looking to showcase Japan-related products, services, or content to audiences from Thailand, Japan, and around the world.

🌸 CONNECT • DISCOVER • CREATE

Discover opportunities to:
• Showcase your products and services to audiences interested in Japan
• Build connections with Thai and Japanese partners
• Explore new business and marketing opportunities
• Create memorable brand experiences and increase brand awareness through a major Japan-focused event in Thailand
